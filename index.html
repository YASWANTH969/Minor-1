OUR TEMPLE

A place of devotion, peace and unity

Sri Rama Temple at Polepalli (Basireddypalli) is a spiritual sanctuary for devotees, families and the local community to come together in prayer and celebration.

Our temple celebrates the values of Lord Sri Rama — dharma, truth, courage, compassion and devotion. This website is created to share temple information, upcoming celebrations, seva opportunities and ways for devotees to support the temple.
