OUR TEMPLE

A Place of Devotion, Peace and Spiritual Unity

Sri Rama Temple at Polepalli (Basireddypalli) is a revered sanctuary where devotees gather to worship Lord Sri Sita Rama, Lakshmana, and Anjaneya Swamy.

Upholding the divine values of Sanatana Dharma, the temple serves as the spiritual heart of the village and surrounding regions in Pulivendula Mandal. Devotees participate in regular pujas, annual anniversaries, and grand Sri Rama Navami celebrations.

DAILY SEVA PANCHANGAM

Daily Rituals & Nitya Sevas (నిత్య సేవా పట్టిక)

Experience the traditional Vedic rituals performed daily at Sri Rama Temple from Suprabhatam to Ekanta Seva.

06:00 AM

🌅 Suprabhatam & Temple Opening

Awakening of Lord Sri Sita Rama with Vedic chants and Suprabhata Seva.

06:30 AM

🪔 Abhishekam & Archana

Morning ritual Abhishekam, Alankaram, and Ashtottara Shatanamarchana with flowers.

08:30 AM

🍚 Morning Naivedyam & Teertha Prasadam

Offering of sacred Naivedyam and distribution of Teertham & Prasadam to devotees.

06:00 PM

🔔 Evening Aarti & Sahasra Deepalankarana

Grand evening oil lamp lighting, Mangala Aarti, Bhajans, and Sahasra Deepa Seva.

08:00 PM

🌙 Ekanta Seva & Pavalimpu Seva

Night Naivedyam, Neerajanam, and Ekanta Seva (Rest ritual) at closing time.
